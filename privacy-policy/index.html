Privacy Policy

How ZAVLO Technologies collects, uses, and safeguards information across our digital interfaces and communications.

Last updated: September 2026
Version: 1.0

1. Overview & Scope

ZAVLO Technologies ("ZAVLO", "we", "us", or "our") develops connected healthcare infrastructure, autonomous medical logistics, and digital platform interfaces. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, and protect information when you visit our website, interact with our platform demonstrations, or submit inquiries regarding our technology.

We believe healthcare infrastructure must be built on trust, transparency, and data integrity. We only collect information strictly required to communicate with you and operate our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us through website inquiry forms, direct email, or messaging channels, as well as limited technical usage data:

  • Contact & Inquiry Data: Full name, institutional or organizational affiliation, email address, inquiry type (such as Deployment, Partnerships, or Technology), and the contents of your message.
  • Technical & Device Data: Standard server log data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, date/time stamps, and viewport resolution for responsiveness.

3. How Information Is Used

The information we collect is used solely for legitimate operational and communication purposes:

  • To respond directly to inquiries regarding system deployment, partnerships, and technical specifications.
  • To evaluate regional and institutional infrastructure requirements for autonomous healthcare systems.
  • To maintain website security, optimize technical performance, and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To fulfill any legal, regulatory, or administrative requirements applicable to our communications.

4. Enquiry Information & WhatsApp Communications

When you initiate an inquiry via our interactive contact form, you have the option to connect through WhatsApp or direct email. Inquiries routed through WhatsApp transmit your formatted message directly to our official communication channels using end-to-end encrypted messaging provided by the WhatsApp platform. We do not store your inquiry on unencrypted intermediaries.

5. Website Usage & Tracking

Our website is designed with a minimal footprint. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising networks or invasive cross-site tracking pixels. Any cookies or local storage utilized are strictly limited to technical functionality, such as user interface preferences and reduced-motion accessibility settings.

6. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We may utilize trusted third-party service providers for website hosting, content delivery networks (CDNs), and secure communications. These providers process technical data strictly on our behalf under confidentiality and security commitments consistent with this policy.

7. External Links & Social Platforms

Our website may contain links to external platforms, including our official Instagram channel (@zavlocare). When navigating to third-party services, their respective privacy policies and terms govern your interactions. We encourage you to review their policies upon visiting.

8. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain communication records only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which they were collected or as required by law.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, operational changes, or legal updates. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our team directly at:
